Enhanced Plotly interaction helpers for Streamlit Components v2. streamlit-plotly-enhance renders Plotly figures in Streamlit and returns cell-level interaction events for heatmap-like charts. It is designed for cases where Streamlit’s native st.plotly_chart event support is not enough, especially heatmap cell click events.
