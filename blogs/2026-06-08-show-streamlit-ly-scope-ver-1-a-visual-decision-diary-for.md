Hi Streamlit community, I built an early Streamlit prototype called LY-Scope Ver.1. It is a personal asset decision app that connects several financial decision inputs into one visual report: Market assets and stock comparison REIT analysis U.S. real estate market view Personal asset statement Scenario stress testing AI coach context Diary-based visual report The core idea is not to provide investment advice.
