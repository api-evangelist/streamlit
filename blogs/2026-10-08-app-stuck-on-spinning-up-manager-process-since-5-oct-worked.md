Hey there, thanks for sharing all those details and welcome to the Streamlit community! You’re not alone—many folks have run into the “Spinning up manager process” issue on Streamlit Community Cloud, especially after a recent push or even when nothing has changed. When the app is reported as running but all requests (including health checks) time out, and rebooting or changing settings doesn’t help, this usually points to a backend orchestration or platform-level issue that only Streamlit staff can resolve—not something you can fix from your end.
