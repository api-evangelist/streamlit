Welcome to the Streamlit community, and thanks for sharing your question and details! This is a known limitation: when you change your GitHub username, Streamlit Community Cloud may not automatically update your displayed username or profile mapping. The platform identifies apps and profiles by the original GitHub coordinates, so even after a successful username change, your public profile and “Created by” badges can still show the old username.
