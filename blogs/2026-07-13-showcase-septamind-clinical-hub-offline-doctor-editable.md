Hi Streamlit Community! I am thrilled to share **SeptaMind Clinical Hub**, a mental health app built specifically to overcome the core infrastructure, privacy, and technical barriers faced by healthtech in real-world clinical settings. Most healthtech tools fail in remote clinics because they require a constant internet connection, rely heavily on cloud-hosted patient data, or act as an uneditable “black box.” SeptaMind fixes this to boost the efficiency of the few mental health professionals in hospitals.
