Hey there, thanks so much for reaching out and for the detailed troubleshooting steps you’ve already tried! You’re definitely not alone—this is a common scenario when the primary email on your GitHub account changes after your Streamlit Community Cloud account was created. Streamlit Community Cloud identifies your account by the email address linked to your GitHub at the time of first sign-in, and sometimes the system doesn’t automatically update when you change your GitHub email, even after revoking and reauthorizing OAuth apps.
