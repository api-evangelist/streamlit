Welcome to the Streamlit community, and thanks for your thoughtful question! My understanding is that for legacy Python dashboards, many developers keep the core business logic (data processing, analysis) and wrap it with a new Streamlit interface, rather than rewriting everything from scratch. This approach leverages existing, battle-tested code while modernizing the UI and adding interactivity.
