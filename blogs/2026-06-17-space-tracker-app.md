A Streamlit app that fetches data from NASA JPL Horizons to pull real-time data for celestial objects, including distance to Earth/Sun, illumination percentage, and phase angles. It is currently hardcoded to a specific location (Weddington, NC) for local use, hosted at weddingtonspacetracker.streamlit.app.
