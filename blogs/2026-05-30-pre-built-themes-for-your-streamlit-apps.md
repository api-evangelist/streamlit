Hey community, the st_yled package (v0.5) now contains a little new feature that lets you try out and apply different theming options for your app. Simply call st_yled.init(theme = ".....") on your app page to load one of the available theme templates. Each theme comes with dark and light mode, so you can rapidly test different moods for your app.
