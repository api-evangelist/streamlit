Hey there, thanks for the detailed question and welcome to the Streamlit community! Sorry to hear about your lost predictions data—that’s always a tough situation. To answer your questions directly: Streamlit Community Cloud does not keep any recoverable snapshot, backup, or cache of your app’s local filesystem after a reboot or redeploy.
