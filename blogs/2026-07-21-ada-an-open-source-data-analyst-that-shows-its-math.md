Drop in a CSV or Excel file. ADA cleans it, builds a dashboard, flags anomalous periods, projects a guarded forecast, and answers plain-English questions about your data — chat included. The difference: every chart, every forecast, every chat answer traces back to a pandas calculation you can actually read.
