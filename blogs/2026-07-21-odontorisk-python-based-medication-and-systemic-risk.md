Hi everyone, I would like to share OdontoRisk , a Python-based Streamlit application for medication and systemic-risk screening in dental care. OdontoRisk was developed as a clinical decision-support prototype to help dental professionals organize patient medications, allergies, systemic conditions, lifestyle factors, and optional vital signs into structured alerts before dental treatment. The app also generates a PDF report for clinical documentation.
