Artificial intelligence is becoming a practical part of modern applications rather than a standalone technology reserved for experimental projects. Businesses are adding AI-powered search, recommendations, chatbots, automation, document processing, predictive analytics, copilots, and intelligent workflows to existing products. When planning an AI initiative, one of the first decisions is whether to integrate AI into an existing application or build a completely new AI-powered application from scratch .
