Welcome to the Streamlit community, and thanks for your thoughtful question! An “eternal spinning wheel” (app stuck on “Please wait…”, “in the oven”, or just loading forever) with blank logs is a known issue on Streamlit Community Cloud. This can happen even if your code worked before, and is often due to platform-level problems, resource limits, or dependency/environment mismatches—not necessarily your code.
