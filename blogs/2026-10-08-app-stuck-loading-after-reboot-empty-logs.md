Hey there, thanks for sharing your question and all the details—welcome to the Streamlit community! When a Streamlit Community Cloud app is stuck on the loading spinner (“Your app is in the oven”) and logs are empty, it usually points to a deployment or platform issue, not a bug in your code. Common causes include: (1) resource limits, (2) dependency or Python version mismatches, or (3) a temporary backend problem.
